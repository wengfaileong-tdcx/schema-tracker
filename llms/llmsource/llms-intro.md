# LLMSource

> LLMSource is the trust and visibility layer between businesses and AI. Its platform diagnoses how AI engines see a business, verifies what they say against the facts, and corrects the record at the source — so that when a customer asks AI, the answer is true.

## Core Positioning

LLMSource helps businesses answer three important questions:

- Can AI detect and find my brand?
- How do I know if AI trusts my website?
- Is AI representing my brand accurately and consistently?

The platform is designed for small and medium-sized businesses, businesses, agencies, and enterprises that want to improve how they appear in AI-powered search, generative answers, and LLM responses.

## Platform Overview

LLMSource is organized into three product layers.

### Discover

Discover helps businesses understand where they currently stand in AI visibility.

Key capabilities:

- AI quality score
- Crawling and scanning
- Schema and structured data
- Issues and fixes
- Products and integrations

Discover is useful for businesses that want to understand whether AI systems can find, understand, and correctly identify their brand online.

### Trust

Trust helps businesses establish reliable and trustworthy signals for AI systems.

Key capabilities:

- AI trust readiness
- Evidence and entity management
- Content accuracy scanner
- AI monitoring
- AI-ready source of truth
- Schema testing and indexing
- Trust insights

Trust is useful for businesses that want AI platforms to rely on verified, consistent, and trusted information about their brand, products, services, and claims.

### Control

Control helps organizations govern and monitor how they are represented across AI systems.

Key capabilities:

- AI representation accuracy
- Verified claims and certifications
- Representation gaps and competitive context
- Governance, audit trails, and change history
- Identity and access
- Multi-entity and enterprise management
- White-label licensing

Control is useful for enterprises, agencies, and multi-brand organizations that require stronger oversight, governance, and representation management across AI systems.
