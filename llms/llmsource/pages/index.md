### How does LLMSource track AI mentions?

We run a standard set of questions about your business against ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, then check the answers against your verified record. Runs repeat on a set cadence, tighter on higher tiers, so you can see when a fact drifts or a mention slips.

### What makes this different from regular SEO tools?

SEO tools track search engines. LLMSource tracks how AI engines describe and represent your brand. We also help define the trusted business signals AI relies on.
