LLMSource is the trust and visibility layer between businesses and AI. Its platform diagnoses how AI engines see a business, verifies what they say against the facts, and corrects the record at the source. It scans a business's website and structured data, produces three scored assessments (AI Quality Score, AI Trust Score, AI Representation Score), and tracks how AI systems describe the business over time — organized into three product layers: Discover, Trust, and Control, detailed below.
