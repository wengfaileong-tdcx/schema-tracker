### What is Trust?

Trust is the second layer of LLMSource. [Discover](https://llmsource.com/products/discover) gets your site readable by AI. Trust then checks whether AI gets your facts right. It verifies your business claims, maps your brand entities, scans your page content for contradictions, and tests how accurately AI answers questions about your business. The result is your AI Trust Score, plus a clear list of what to fix first.

### What is an AI trust signals audit?

It’s a check of how accurately AI answers real questions about your business. LLMSource asks the questions, compares each answer to your verified facts, and scores the results. We ask each question several times, because an answer AI gets right only sometimes is still a risk, one you should see, not one that gets smoothed over. We only score facts your profile has verified, so you’re never judged on something you haven’t confirmed. The audit shows where trust is weak, so you know what to fix.

### Why does accurate AI representation matter?

More customers now ask AI about your business before they ever visit your site. If AI gets your hours, location, or credentials wrong, you lose trust and sales before anyone reaches you. A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer at all. Trust finds those errors, sorts them by how much they matter, and helps you fix them so AI gets you right.

### Who is Trust for?

Trust works for any business AI describes to customers, from small teams to large brands. If customers ask AI about your hours, location, products, or credentials before visiting, this is for you. The AI Trust Score starts on Pro, so [small teams](https://llmsource.com/solutions/smb) can see how accurately AI describes them. [Business](https://llmsource.com/solutions/business) and [Enterprise](https://llmsource.com/solutions/enterprise) include the tools to act on it. [Agency](https://llmsource.com/solutions/agency) gets them through the AI Representation Score add-on.

### Do I need Trust if I already use Discover?

Discover makes your site readable by AI. Trust checks whether AI gets your facts right once it can read you. A site can pass Discover, with clean schema and no scan errors, and still fail Trust because its claims are unproven or its pages contradict each other. Trust measures and fixes what AI tells your customers, not just whether it can read your site.

### Do I need to understand schema or entities to use this?

No. Schema is the hidden code that labels your pages so AI can read them. An entity is anything AI links back to you, like your company, a location, or a product. Your job in Trust isn’t technical: you state facts, back them with evidence, and confirm what’s correct. Trust then flags where AI’s answers disagree with what you’ve verified. Anything that needs a schema change goes to Discover, where the fix arrives as ready-to-copy code.

### What results can I expect?

Results vary by platform. AI engines that pull from live sources can reflect a fix within days, but those that rely on their own training data only catch up when the provider updates the model, and that’s out of your hands.

### Will this change my website automatically?

No. Trust measures accuracy and flags issues, but it doesn’t touch your live site. You decide what to verify, what to publish, and when. Schema fixes go through Discover, where you review them first.

### What happens if my site or facts change later?

Trust keeps checking. Each scan rechecks your claims and page content, and the AI-ready data feed refreshes after every crawl and whenever you verify a new claim. If a site update introduces a contradiction, it gets flagged.

### Where is my data stored, and how is it handled?

Your claims, evidence, and scan data live in your LLMSource account. The data feed you publish is public, so AI can read accurate facts about you, but you decide what goes into it. [Enterprise’s](https://llmsource.com/solutions/enterprise) [Control](https://llmsource.com/products/control) layer adds region-specific storage, PII masking, and stricter access controls. Standard account storage covers day-to-day use for most teams.
