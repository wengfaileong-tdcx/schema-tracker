### How has search changed?

Search engines used to guess what a page was about from meta tags, keywords, and links, then handed you a list of blue links to sort through. AI systems work differently. They read your schema, the structured data that spells out what your business is, what you sell, and how to reach you, and answer the question directly. That’s why clean, complete schema now matters as much as your page copy, and why teams are turning to AI discovery tools instead of traditional SEO checkers alone.

### What is Discover?

Discover is the first layer of LLMSource. It checks whether AI can find and read your website. It scans your pages, checks your structured data, flags what’s blocking them, and then gives you an AI Quality Score from 0 to 100. A low score means AI systems struggle to read your site. A high score means they can read it correctly.

### Why does it matter whether AI can read my site?

More people now ask AI for recommendations instead of scrolling through search results. If AI can’t read your site correctly, it may skip your business or get the details wrong, no matter how good your content is. Discover runs a full AI visibility audit to find those gaps and shows you how to close them, so AI has the full picture when it answers questions about you.

### Who is Discover for?

Discover is for anyone who wants AI engines to find and describe their business correctly, no technical background needed. [Solo owners](https://llmsource.com/solutions/smb) on Starter use it to get a first score and fix obvious issues. [Agencies](https://llmsource.com/solutions/agency) and larger teams use it to manage many sites at once. It’s included on every plan.

### Do I need to understand schema or code to use this?

No. Schema, also called structured data, is background code that tells AI what each part of your page means, such as your business name, hours, or product prices. Discover reads that code for you and explains what it found in plain English.

### How does the scan work?

You enter your website address, and Discover scans your public pages. It crawls what’s there, finds your JSON-LD (the code behind your structured data), and identifies which schema types you’re using. Then it checks that code against schema rules and shows you exactly where structured data is missing, broken, or incomplete, page by page.

### Will Discover automatically change my website?

Not without you choosing to. Discover flags each issue and shows what needs to change, but nothing goes live until you apply it. From Pro up, you get plain-English recommendations with code you can copy and use or hand to a developer. Connect a supported e-commerce platform and you can apply a recommended schema fix in one click. Your trial includes one of these, so you can try it before you commit.

### Can I prove what was scanned and fixed?

Yes, to a point. Schema version history logs every change to your structured data. Fix verification re-scans your site after each fix, so you can confirm it worked and watch your score move. That covers day-to-day proof for most teams. For formal proof a board or compliance team needs, Control adds a full, tamper-evident audit log.

### What does Discover not do?

Discover checks whether AI can technically read your site, but it doesn’t confirm whether AI gets your facts right or track whether AI recommends you in category answers. Those are separate layers. [Trust](https://llmsource.com/products/trust) checks what AI says about your business against your verified profile, so you can see when AI gets a fact wrong. [Control](https://llmsource.com/products/control) goes a step further and probes live AI engines to see how they actually represent your business, and whether that stays accurate over time. Discover is the foundation both build on.
