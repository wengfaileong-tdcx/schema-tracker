### How is this different from an AI visibility monitoring tool?

Monitoring tells you what AI says about your client. LLMSource also fixes the underlying schema, content, and trust signals, then deploys those fixes to the client’s site. That turns a monitoring chart into billable delivery work, so the same retainer covers more output.

### Is everything truly white-label?

Yes. Reports, the Client Portal add-on, scheduled PDFs, the data feed, and the API responses all carry your agency’s logo, colors, and custom domain, not LLMSource. We stay invisible and you stay the expert your clients are paying for.

### What does white-label look like in practice for reports?

You configure your logo, brand colors, agency name, and contact details once. Three PDF report types are available: Full Audit (everything), Score Summary (executive view), and Issues Only (technical detail). Each can be scheduled weekly or monthly per client. The PDFs carry no LLMSource branding, no watermark, no footer mention.

### How does LLMSource track whether AI is representing a client accurately?

The AI Answer Checker runs live queries against ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity and shows how each one describes the client: what’s accurate, what’s wrong, and what’s missing. Claims & Evidence then lets you correct the record with verified facts and sources, which lifts the client’s AI Trust Score over time.

### Does it work with Jira, Zendesk, or other ticketing tools?

Yes. Bi-directional Jira sync (issue creation, status updates) and Zendesk integration are available on Agency as add-ons, and native on Business and above. When a critical issue is detected, a Jira ticket is auto-created and routed to your delivery team. Status changes sync back into LLMSource automatically.

### What if a client’s site runs on Drupal, Umbraco, or another CMS not on the native list?

LLMSource natively supports Shopify, WordPress, Wix, AEM, Sitecore, Contentful, and Webflow. For anything else, like Drupal, Umbraco, Magento, or a custom build, fixes export as clean JSON-LD your dev team can drop in, and CSV upload covers catalog data on every plan. Need a native integration? Contact us and we’ll scope a custom build or add it to the roadmap.

### Does it come with API access?

You get a full read-and-write REST API on the Agency tier. Pull AI Quality Scores, AI Trust Scores, citation data, and issue lists for any client into your reporting pipeline. Push approvals, trigger crawls, and deploy fixes in code. Rate limits scale with the number of client projects, and you authenticate with API keys per workspace.

### What if a client wants to leave or switch agencies?

You own the relationship and the data. Client projects can be transferred between agency workspaces, or exported as JSON with full scan history, fix logs, and report archives. If a client wants direct access, they can sign up their own LLMSource account and you transfer ownership. There’s no lock-in and no exit fee.
