### Who built LLMSource?

LLMSource delivers AI-powered solutions backed by human expertise. With a focus on pairing technology with real-world business needs, LLMSource is more than a quick technical fix: it’s built to help businesses improve, manage, and protect how AI represents them over time.

### Why should I trust a new platform with something this important?

You don’t have to take our word for it. Run your first scan for free and see the results in minutes. From there, every fix comes with a clear before-and-after, so you can see exactly what changed and verify it yourself.

### How is this different from SEO?

Traditional SEO is about ranking a page in a list of results. LLM visibility is about getting your brand named or cited inside a synthesized AI answer. A page can rank first on Google and never appear when someone asks ChatGPT the same question.

### Is my data safe with LLMSource?

Yes. Your data is protected when it is sent and stored, access is limited to authorized users, and integrations only request the permissions they need. We don't sell or share your data, and payments are processed through Stripe using PCI-compliant infrastructure. Enterprise customers also get added controls. That includes choosing whether data is stored in the EU, US, or APAC, managing encryption through their own key management system, and exporting a full SOC 2 audit trail for compliance.
