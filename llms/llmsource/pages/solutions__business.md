### Why not just buy an AI monitoring tool and add it to our existing SEO stack?

Monitoring tells you what AI says. It doesn’t fix anything. The Business tier closes the loop with visibility, remediation, and attribution under one platform with RBAC, so marketing, dev, and SEO teams work from the same data instead of three disconnected tools.

### How does score-to-traffic attribution work?

LLMSource connects to GA4 and Search Console, then correlates your AI Quality Score and AI Trust Score lift against organic sessions over time. The Marketing ROI View renders it as a plain-English summary that can be read in minutes, instead of a chart needing interpretation.

### We’re a cross-functional team across marketing, dev, and SEO. How does adoption work?

Each role gets its own view and its own alerts. The marketing team sees the ROI dashboard; the dev team sees the technical health board; the SEO Lead sees both plus citation share. Approval workflows mean no one ships changes without sign-off.

### Can we customize the role permissions beyond the 6 built-in roles?

Business comes with six built-in roles: Admin, Project Manager, Editor, Developer, Auditor, External Partner. Each has its own permission matrix. Custom roles via the Permission Matrix are an Enterprise feature, where the governance committee can define exactly what each role can see and approve on a per-action basis.

### How do approval workflows work for fix deployment?

Dev proposes a fix. The system routes it to whichever role you’ve designated as approver, typically the marketing team or the SEO Lead. They review the before-and-after JSON-LD preview in LLMSource, approve or reject with a comment, and the fix deploys on approval. Every action is logged. No one deploys changes alone.

### How does Slack, Teams, and Google Chat routing actually decide who gets what alert?

Each alert type is mapped to a role, not a channel. Score drops route to the SEO Lead. Approval requests route to the Marketing Director or whoever you’ve designated. Critical issues route to the dev team. You can route by project, severity, or score threshold. Alerts land where action can happen, not in noise.

### What ad platforms do you support, and how deep does the integration go?

Google Ads and Meta Ads are included on Business. TikTok Ads or Custom integrations are available as a $99/mo add-on for each. The dashboard pulls campaign performance, CPCs, and conversion data alongside your AI search scores. ### Can we export data to our BI tool or data warehouse?

Yes. Business includes full REST API access for reading and writing every data point: scores, issues, fixes, citations, traffic correlation. Common patterns include nightly exports to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Looker via your existing ETL pipeline. Webhooks fire on score changes, critical issues, and approval requests.
