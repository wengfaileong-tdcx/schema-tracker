### What’s the difference between SEO and AI discoverability?

SEO helps you show up on search results. AI discoverability optimizes for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini to read your store, validate your claims, and recommend your products.

### How does this work with my Shopify, WordPress, or Wix store?

Native integrations sync your catalog in 1–5 minutes. These are available in [Agency](https://llmsource.com/solutions/agency), [Business](https://llmsource.com/solutions/business), and [Enterprise](https://llmsource.com/solutions/enterprise) plans, and as an add-on for $49/mo with the Pro plan. However, even without an integration, you can still upload products via CSV on any plan. Fixes deploy back to your store with one approval click.

### What if my store is on a different platform aside from Shopify, WordPress, or Wix?

LLMSource natively supports Shopify, WordPress, and Wix today. For other platforms, including regional marketplaces, you can use CSV upload and manual schema export work for these platforms. If you need a different integration, let us know and we can provide a custom build.

### Does it work with a headless or custom storefront?

Yes. If your store isn’t on Shopify, WordPress, or Wix, upload your products as a CSV and apply fixes via schema export. LLMSource will provide you with the JSON-LD code for your developer to use.

### What if I’m not technical enough to do this myself?

You don’t need any technical knowledge. LLMSource has in-platform step-by-step guides to help you set up or fix issues. We also draft emails explaining issues and fixes that you can send to your developer. Alternatively, you can also reach out to LLMSource experts to handle the issues for a fee.

### What does ‘schema’ mean?

Schema is a standardized way of organizing information on your site so AI can understand it more accurately and surface your brand and products when relevant.

### What does the live schema check look at?

It checks whether key details are filled in for every product such as GTIN, MPN, brand, price, stock status, ratings, return policy, and shipping info. You’ll see which products are fully ready, partially ready, or invisible to AI, and which to fix first.

### Do I need to add structured data to every product page manually?

No. Once you approve a fix, the JSON-LD code is automatically deployed to your product metafields (description). This works for Shopify, WordPress/WooCommerce, and Wix.

### What happens if I update my store’s design or install a new app?

Sometimes a theme change or a new app install can overwrite product schema. On Starter plans and above, we monitor your fixes and alert you immediately if something gets reversed with a one-click option to reapply it.
