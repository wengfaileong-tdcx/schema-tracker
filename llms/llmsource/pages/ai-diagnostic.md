### What is an AI Diagnostic?

It’s a report that shows how AI assistants describe your business to customers today. It shows how often they name you, where competitors are named instead, and what your website is missing, then gives you clear recommendations on what to do next.

### Which AI assistants do you check?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. We ask each one the questions your customers are likely to ask, then compare the answers with your website and public records.

### What’s included in the report?

Your AI Representation Score (how often, how high and how positively AI names you), your AI Quality Score (how clearly your website states the facts AI needs), recommendations ranked by impact, paste-ready code for your web team and a 30-day plan.

### Can you guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No one can control what AI assistants say. The diagnostic shows where AI leaves you out and what is most likely to help, so you can act on the clearest opportunities first and track the change over time.

### Does it work with any website?

Yes. Our recommendations work whether your website runs on a CMS, a website builder or custom code.

### How is this different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in search results. This diagnostic looks at what AI assistants actually say about your business when customers ask them directly, an area often called AEO (answer engine optimization) or GEO (generative engine optimization).

### How do I check what AI says about my business?

Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, then note whether you’re named, how you’re described and who’s named instead. The AI Diagnostic Report does this for you: it asks each assistant the questions your customers are likely to ask and brings the answers together in one report.

### How can I optimize my website for AI search?

Make the facts AI looks for easy to find and consistent. State your services, location, hours and contact details clearly on your key pages, add structured data (schema markup), keep your details the same across your website and listings, and answer the questions customers actually ask in plain language. The AI Diagnostic Report shows which facts are missing or unclear on your website and ranks the fixes by impact.
